      03 What the customer sees

      The same case, inside the bank app.

      The customer we interviewed said trust comes from seeing the case in the app and not having to explain again. Identity is confirmed here, never by reading a code down the phone.

      Why the approval lives here

      The agent asks for an approval inside the app and never for a PIN, a password or a one time code. A customer who has been taught to refuse those questions can still verify safely, and the same request is visible in the app they already trust.

      From the customer interview, Dubai, 22 September
      What happened to him
      Explained the delayed salary five times, to two collectors, the complaints team, the assessor and the agency. Calls kept coming for three to four days after he complained.
      What this build does
      He explains once. The case, the pause and the named owner appear in his app, and the agency dialer is refused from the same list.

      "What would make me trust it is seeing the same case in my bank app and not having to explain myself again."

        Still out of scope, and named as such

        Bereavement and serious illness routing, self-harm escalation, deceased and insolvency cases, and accent bias testing. Both collections heads raised these. They belong in a pilot, designed with qualified people, not bolted onto a prototype.

        Arabic and Urdu packs are drafted for this prototype and marked pending review by a fluent speaker and the institution's compliance reviewer. No pilot runs on unreviewed regulated wording.
