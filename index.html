    What the customer sees

    The same case, inside the bank app.

    The customer we interviewed said trust comes from seeing the case in the app and not having to explain again. Identity is confirmed here, never by reading a code down the phone.

    Why this matters

    The agent asks the customer to approve a request inside the app. It never asks for a PIN, a password or a one time code, so a customer who has been taught to refuse those questions can still verify safely.

    The approval confirms identity only. It never moves money.
