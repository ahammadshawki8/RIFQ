# Demo script

Three minutes, one browser tab, no keys. Open https://ahammadshawki8.github.io/RIFQ/

## Scene 1: the call that never happens (30 seconds)

> "Before anything dials, the gate decides."

- Drag the clock to **20:00**. Every card flips to blocked.
- Open the check list on a blocked card: consent, calling window, attempt limits, opt-outs, open cases, policy in date.
- Point at **Layla**: opted out. At **Ravi**: called twice already today.

> "A blocked call never reaches telephony, and the reason is written down."

## Scene 2: the routine call (45 seconds)

- Set the clock back to **11:00**, call **Imran Qureshi**.
- Answer *"Yes, speaking"*. Note that nothing about the account has been said yet.
- Say *"Can we speak in Urdu?"* The agent switches to the approved Urdu pack and the workflow state does not move.
- Open **Customer app**, press **Approve**. No PIN, no OTP.
- Back in the call: *"I can pay on the 12th"*, then pick one of the dates the server offered.

> "The dates come from the bank, not from the model. The reference is read back, and the customer has it in writing."

## Scene 3: the call that matters (60 seconds)

- Return to the gate and call **Sara Haddad**, who speaks Arabic.
- Verify in the app, then choose *"I lost my job last week."*

Point at three things on screen as they happen:

1. The collection tools strike out and are marked **removed**.
2. A case opens: **named owner, four hour SLA**.
3. **Automated contact paused**, and the banner says the agency file export reads the same list.

- Open **Customer app**: the case, the pause, and who owns it, in the customer's own view.
- Return to the gate: Sara is now blocked. Press **Agency attempts to dial**: refused.

> "This is the part both collections heads said was the real product. Detection was never the hard bit."

## Scene 4: proof (30 seconds)

- Open **Evidence**, click any sentence the agent spoke.
- Show the approved template id, the language, the policy version, the approver and the hash.
- Show a refused tool call in the receipts table.
- Open **Test results**: 68 checks, three runs per scenario, reported per language.

> "Every regulated sentence traces to an approved template and a policy version. Not a promise, a receipt."

## Closing line

> "RIFQ does not ask an AI to behave. It makes approved behaviour the only behaviour available, and proves what happened."

## If you only have 60 seconds

Scene 3, then Scene 4.
